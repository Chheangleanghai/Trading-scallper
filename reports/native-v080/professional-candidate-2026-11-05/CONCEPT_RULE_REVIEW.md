# A–H rule review — 2026-11-05

This review separates the code's operational definitions, external teaching references and measured results. The references below explain methods; none validates these exact XAUUSD rules, costs, targets or parameters. No external method's profitability claim is adopted.

## What is actually implemented

| Module | Confirmed entry sequence in this project | Assessment and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · Sweep/MSS/FVG | A previously confirmed pivot is swept and reclaimed; a later directional, displaced close breaks the frozen opposite level and forms a three-candle FVG; a still later gap retest closes directionally through its boundary. | Explicit, strict reversal model. It combines MSS and FVG on the displacement bar and then requires a later retest. It does not encode every discretionary ICT narrative or automatically trade every displayed FVG. |
| B · CRT | Freeze the previous completed reference candle. Sweep one boundary and close back inside its range, then wait for a later close beyond the sweep candle's trigger. | A defined range-reversal approximation. Default targets are fixed 1/2/3R, **not automatically the CRT midpoint/opposite range boundary**. That is a material difference from the range-targeting framework described in the creator documentation cited below. |
| C · Confirmed-pivot Turtle Soup | Wait until a confirmed pivot has the configured age; price breaks it with a wick and closes back across it; a later directional trigger close is required. | The source explicitly describes a pivot-based false-breakout variant. It is **not the original 20-day Turtle Soup** and is not a body-break/return “Turtle Body Soup” definition. A wick-only sweep is not mislabeled as proof of a body-break setup. |
| D · K-SNR display | At least two confirmed pivots form a horizontal cluster within ATR tolerance. A displaced breakout creates a later retest setup; a rejection/failed breakout creates a later directional break setup. | The existing confirmed support/resistance rule is preserved. **K-SNR is the requested display name.** No authoritative, specific K-SNR rulebook was supplied or located, so this review cannot certify that this is a particular proprietary/local K-SNR method. Internal wire ID `D_SNR` remains stable. |
| E · Supply/demand | An opposite candle precedes a displaced close through a known confirmed swing; freeze that candle's full range, then require a later directional zone retest. Invalidation and a tested-bar cap apply. | A bounded price-action zone heuristic. Counting touched bars is not the same as counting separate multi-bar visits. It does not prove institutional orders or reproduce every discretionary base/departure methodology. |
| F · Structural trend pullback | Confirmed chart structure and EMA9/21 direction, optionally EMA50, establish trend. A pullback reaches the fast/slow band without excessive ATR overshoot; a later directional break of the frozen pullback candle confirms entry. | A structural pullback model, not an unconditional EMA crossover. EMA55 is visual only; changing its display does not substitute it for the strategy's EMA50. |
| G · AMD approximation | A prior rolling range is compressed relative to ATR; sweep/reclaim one edge, then require later displacement through the opposite frozen edge. | A rolling-range approximation. It does not model full session-specific accumulation/manipulation/distribution, volume absorption, or the complete Wyckoff phase/test framework. |
| H · Confirmed Quasimodo approximation | Five alternating confirmed pivots define shoulder/head geometry; the stored closing neckline break must occur after head confirmation and no later than right-shoulder confirmation; a later retest of the first-shoulder level triggers. | A bounded five-pivot approximation. Simultaneously confirmed high/low pivots are excluded from sequencing. No primary RTM lesson was available to certify all of that school's discretionary qualifications. |

The relevant code is in `updateCandidates()`, the pure pattern predicates, `Candidate.begin/maintain/confirmBreak/confirmRetest`, and the common arbitration block in the generated candidates. Parent-reconstruction tests verify that these rules are unchanged by this UI/default upgrade. This establishes implementation continuity; it does not prove that any chosen methodology is the uniquely correct interpretation of its name.

## Why a visible pattern can have no signal

FVG, OB, swing and sweep **context overlays are independent display annotations**. A context FVG can use a different displacement test from module A's full sweep → MSS/FVG → later retest sequence. Seeing that overlay does not mean a qualified entry was rejected incorrectly.

Every candidate must also pass the actual common filters: allowed direction/date window; structural invalidation; target availability and ordering; optional location filters; ATR volatility; manual exclusion; session; H1 alignment; optional EMA alignment; stop/entry distance; minimum TP2 R; score; cooldown; session quota; and an available plan slot. Opposing qualified directions reject. The highest-scoring eligible candidate wins; ties use the existing array order.

Pivots become available after their right-hand confirmation bars. With strength three on M5, that confirmation alone requires three later five-minute bars. Module-specific later confirmation/retest adds more latency. The project uses confirmed closes and does not claim an instantaneous intrabar predictor. Enabling A–H broadens the candidate set without removing these safeguards.

The native final card reports **Session plan limit** after five accepted London plans. That is direct evidence of the current blocking reason. The broader-session sample reached the same five-plan cap on 16 of 17 complete trading dates. Raising the quota, dropping H1 alignment or lowering the quality threshold would be separate research changes; none was silently applied here.

## Method references and what they support

- [The Inner Circle Trader — 2022 Mentorship, Episode 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bkt8B3kLATQ) is an original creator reference for the MSS/FVG topic. The accessible page metadata does not provide a complete transcript; this pass does not claim to have audited every discretionary rule from the video. The code's exact A conditions are explicitly stated above.
- [CandelaCharts' own CRT model documentation](https://docs.candelacharts.com/models/crt-model-tm/overview) describes its range anchor, sweep/reclaim and opposite-boundary framework. It is primary documentation for that creator's implementation, not evidence of a universal CRT standard or the performance of this project's fixed-R implementation.
- [Linda Bradford Raschke's entry-strategy presentation hosted by ATAA](https://nwe.net.au/trading/Download%20Files/ATAA/Times%20Are%20Changing/Times%20are%20Changing%20-%20Entry%20Strategies.pdf) was located during research; retrieval was unreliable. It is retained as an author-reference lead, not used to claim a complete primary-source audit of the original Turtle Soup chapter. The project's own source already explicitly disclaims the original 20-day variant.
- [CME's support/resistance lesson](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/technical-analysis/support-and-resistance) discusses prior highs/lows, zones and role reversal. It supports describing D as confirmed support/resistance; it does not establish a specific K-SNR method or certify the two-touch/ATR parameters.
- [CME's moving-average lesson](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/technical-analysis/understanding-moving-averages) explains EMA smoothing and common trend/support-resistance uses. It does not validate this exact structural pullback, target scheme or XAUUSD expectancy.
- [Wyckoff Analytics' method tutorial](https://www.wyckoffanalytics.com/wyckoff-method/) includes price/volume tests, trading ranges and accumulation/distribution phases. G's rolling price/ATR range omits substantial parts of that framework and is therefore kept explicitly labeled as an approximation.
- [Read the Market](https://readthemarket.com/) was checked as a creator source; a sufficiently specific accessible Quasimodo lesson was not located. H remains an explicitly documented approximation. No canonical certification or profitability claim is substituted from an unrelated broker/marketing article.

No authoritative K-SNR definition was established from the supplied spelling alone. An exact rule source/example would be needed before implementing a distinct K-SNR method. No new K-SNR trading rules were invented.

## Effectiveness: actual evidence is insufficient for an edge

The new source-selected M5 Strategy logs contain 74 complete positions, 31 winners, 43 losers, gross +$40.366 before commission and net **−$88.602272** after $128.968272 commission. The complete-position profit factor is **0.745150**. One partially closed position remains separate. See [the actual outcome data](strategy-final-outcomes.json) and [the capture review](README.md).

Positive C/D subgroups are small and selected by a combined-module arbitration process. B dominates the sample. A/G have no selected plan in it. These facts do not establish independent module efficacy, the superiority of one concept, or calibrated quality scores. No threshold or module was selected retrospectively to turn the report positive. More frequent plans are not evidence of better net returns.

These experiments are not represented as completed native results in this pass. Free-plan intraday Replay and genuine future OOS remain outside the completed evidence.
